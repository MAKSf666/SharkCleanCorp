GOOD TO KNOW

A few clean answers.

Can I book a one-time cleaning?

Yes. Explore standard, deep and move-in / move-out cleaning. Discuss the scope and available appointments before booking.

How is the price determined?

Your quote depends on the home's size, condition and the work requested. The price is agreed before the cleaning begins.

Can I buy cleaning products separately?

The cleaning collection is a separate part of our offering. Explore All Purpose Cleaner, Bathroom Foam Spray, Glass Cleaner and Cleaning Paste above. Prices and ordering information will be added here.

What should I do before the visit?

Put away personal items, arrange access and tell us about any surfaces or areas that need special care. We'll agree on the rest together.
